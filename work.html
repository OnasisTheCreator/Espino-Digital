Business Systems Custom App Operations Live Production

Trucking Operations Dashboard

A trucking owner-operator was managing load offers, broker communications, and dispatch decisions across email, texts, and multiple spreadsheets. Nothing talked to each other. We built a single internal tool that pulls everything into one place and surfaces what actually needs attention.

The Problem

Data spread across too many places

Load offers came in through email. Broker rates were in one spreadsheet. Booked loads in another. Revenue estimates in a third. Making a good decision required pulling from all of them simultaneously, which meant decisions were slow or just guessed at.

The Build

Custom web app with live data aggregation

We built a purpose-built internal tool that pulls load offers directly from Gmail, aggregates load board data, ranks brokers by actual booking rate and revenue history, and presents everything in a Kanban pipeline from offer to booked to closed. One screen. Full picture.

Interface Modules
  • Inbox view: Load offers parsed and filtered from Gmail automatically
  • Load board: External feed filtered by lane, rate floor, and broker score
  • Broker rankings: Sorted by booking rate and cumulative revenue
  • Kanban pipeline: Drag from offer to negotiating to booked
  • Metrics dashboard: Today's loads, active offers, book rate, top broker performance
What It Solved

One screen instead of five

Every load decision now starts from the same place. The operator has full visibility into what's available, who's offering it, and whether it's worth taking based on that broker's actual track record. Decisions that used to take 20 minutes of context-switching now take 3.

Auto Repair: Website and Follow-up System

A Charlotte auto repair shop had a weak website, no system for missed calls, and almost no Google reviews. Work was coming in through word of mouth but not converting from online search. We rebuilt their digital presence and added automated follow-up that runs without anyone having to manage it.

The Problem

Visible but not converting

People were finding the shop through Google Maps, but the website looked dated and the shop had fewer than 20 reviews against competitors with 200+. Calls were being missed with no follow-up. Spanish-speaking customers had no clear path.

The Build

New site, text-back, review flow

We built a new custom site with clear service structure, bilingual copy, and a layout that leads visitors toward calling or booking. We added missed-call text-back that fires in under 60 seconds, and a post-service review request that goes out automatically after every job closes.

What Was Delivered
  • Custom website in English and Spanish
  • Missed call text-back (fires in under 60 seconds)
  • Bilingual follow-up sequence for cold leads
  • Review request flow triggered after job close
  • Return customer reactivation at 3 and 6 months
What It Solved

Fewer leads disappearing quietly

Missed calls now get an immediate response instead of silence. The review system runs automatically instead of relying on staff to remember to ask. Spanish-speaking customers have a proper experience from first click to follow-up. The shop stopped apologizing for its site when showing it to customers.

Interactive Product Configurator Demo

Garage Door Style Visualizer

An interactive product configurator that lets customers preview garage door styles and colors on a house before making a decision. Built as a standalone tool that can be embedded on any contractor or supplier website. The goal was to reduce indecision and increase order confidence.

The Problem

Customers couldn't visualize the product

Most garage door companies show a catalog of panels in isolation. Customers struggle to picture how a door will actually look on their house. This leads to hesitation, more back-and-forth, and sometimes a decision to just keep the old door.

The Build

CSS and JavaScript configurator, no 3D engine

Built entirely with CSS and vanilla JavaScript, no three.js or WebGL required. The house, roof, and door are all rendered in CSS. Color and style changes animate in real time. Embeds in a single script tag. Works on mobile. Runs fast.

Capabilities
  • 4 panel styles: Raised, Flat, Carriage, Modern
  • 8 color options with live preview
  • Smooth CSS transitions between selections
  • Fully responsive, works on any screen size
  • Embeddable in any HTML page
What It Shows

Interactive tools reduce hesitation

When customers can see the product in context, the decision becomes easier. This same pattern applies to paint colors, window styles, roofing materials, landscaping, or any product where the visual matters as much as the spec.
